TEPADINA® Dose Preparation Guide (Singapore)

For Healthcare Professionals only
Important: The TEPADINA® 400mg MULTICHAMBER BAG (MCB) must be activated immediately upon removal from refrigeration. To activate MCB, press firmly on the centre portion of the bag (the solvent chamber) to apply uniform pressure, then mix vigorously.

Dose Calculation Parameters

Dose range: 200mg to 1000mg in 1mg increments

TEPADINA® Preparation Steps

Prescribed Dose:
260 mg
Dosing Frequency:
OD for 2 days
Materials Required:
1 MCB, 2 vials

Abbreviations

OD: Once daily

BID: Bis in die (twice a day)

MCB: MULTICHAMBER BAG

PVC: Polyvinyl Chloride

EMA: European Medicines Agency

Adverse events should be reported to: safety@axxessbio.com

This guide is for Singapore use only. Refer to Singapore PI for complete prescribing information.

Stability data: Activated MCB stable for 48 hours at 2-8°C (Singapore PI). EMA reports 168 hours stability.

Mixed MCB and vials stable for 24 hours at 2-8°C.
